# The Memo
The dashboard reported that approximately half of all matches ended in failure, leading to the quarterly ranked-mode rewards being frozen. However, my investigation found **only 12 instances** of a player experiencing a network timeout across 200 player participation records. These incidents occurred across several matches, but a player disconnecting does not necessarily mean the entire match failed. Additionally, the available data does not include a match-level status indicating whether a match completed successfully or failed, so the reported failure rate cannot be verified from the available information alone.

The dashboard's original calculation was not provided, so I cannot confirm the source of the discrepancy. One possibility is that the calculation mishandled missing information. For example, a calculation intended to identify records without a network timeout might have silently excluded records where the disconnect reason was unknown. If those excluded records were then handled incorrectly in the failure-rate calculation, the reported percentage could be misleading. This is only a possible explanation, not a confirmed cause; the original calculation would need to be reviewed to determine what went wrong.

I recommend that your team review the dashboard's calculation, clearly define what qualifies as a failed match, and verify the failure rate against data that actually records match outcomes. Until that review is complete, the reported figure should not be treated as confirmed evidence that half of all matches failed. The quarterly payout should be reconsidered based on the verified results rather than remain frozen solely because of an unvalidated dashboard figure.
